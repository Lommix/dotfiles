Start at least 3 code-reviewer agents reviewing the current diff.
Communicate the original task and intent of the change. Confirm their findings and fix critical issues.

Depending on the Task, these are the different perspectives:

1. Correctness
2. Alignment
3. Over engineering
4. Security
5. Edge cases
